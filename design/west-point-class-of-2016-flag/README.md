# West Point Class of 2016 – flag (3 × 5 ft)

Black flag with a gold frame, arched "WEST POINT", the Army West Point shield in the middle and "CLASS OF 2016"
beneath, all in Army gold. Built to match the black / gold tailgate flag and backdrop.

## Specs

- Trim 60 × 36 in, bleed 1 in. Gold frame 0.8 in thick, inset 2 in from the trim, so a 1 in hem keeps it clear.
- "WEST POINT" 48 in wide on a 50 in radius arch; shield 13 in tall; "CLASS OF 2016" 40 in wide.
- Lettering: Alfa Slab One (open licence), converted to outlines. Shield: vector trace of the supplied Army West Point logo, one-color gold.
- Colors: gold `#D3BC8D` (Army gold, RGB 211 / 188 / 141, Pantone 467 C); field black `#0A0A0A` with a soft lift to `#1F1F1F` at the centre.

## Ordering notes

- This one carries text, so order **double-sided (2-ply with blockout)** if it flies on a pole and will be seen from both sides; on a single-reverse flag the lettering reads backwards from the back.
- If it hangs on the tent or a fence like the reference banner, single-sided with brass grommets at the four corners is fine and cheaper. The frame is inset enough for corner grommets.
- Matte polyester, dye-sublimation; ask the printer to match Pantone 467 C for the gold.
- Confirm the shield may be used under the sponsorship / class-use guidelines.
